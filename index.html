Hair Care FAQs

Common hair problems, answered

Most issues people come in with aren't about the haircut itself. They're about texture, scalp and growth patterns. These are the questions we hear most in the chair.

01My hairline is receding or thinning. What haircut should I get?+

Temples pulling back or a sparse crown make long, heavy styles look thinner than they really are.

How we fix itShorter sides and a textured top that adds visual density, cut to work with your hairline, not against it.

02How do I get rid of dandruff and a dry, itchy scalp?+

Flaking and itching are often made worse by harsh shampoos, hot water and Danish winters.

How we fix itA scalp check during your consultation, plus honest advice on your washing routine and products that actually help.

03I have a cowlick that won't lie flat. Can a haircut fix it?+

Crowns and fringes that stick up or split usually fight back because the cut ignores the growth direction.

How we fix itWe read the growth pattern and cut the weight so the hair falls naturally with the cowlick instead of against it.

04My curly hair always ends up frizzy and shapeless. What can I do?+

Curly and wavy hair puffs out or loses shape when it's cut like straight hair.

How we fix itCurl-aware cutting and shaping, plus a simple at-home routine for defined, controlled texture.

05How can I avoid razor bumps and ingrown hairs after a fade?+

Red bumps and irritation on the neck and beard line are common after shaving or very close fades.

How we fix itCorrect blade direction, tools sanitised after every client, and a soothing finish for sensitive skin.

06My beard grows in patchy. Can it still look full and tidy?+

Gaps on the cheeks or an uneven neckline can make a beard look untidy even when it's grown out.

How we fix itStructured shaping and a clean line-up that disguise patches and balance your face shape.

07My hair gets greasy and flat by midday. How do I keep volume?+

Oily roots weigh hair down, so it won't hold a style or any volume.

How we fix itA cut that removes bulk at the root, plus matte products that hold without weighing your hair down.

08Why does my haircut only look good for a week?+

If a cut isn't tailored to your head shape and growth, it loses its shape fast as it grows out.

How we fix itA consultation-first cut, built around your face shape, so it keeps looking good between visits.
