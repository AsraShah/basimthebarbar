The questions we hear most in the chair

Good hair starts
with a good
why.

Most issues people come in with aren't about the haircut itself. They're about texture, scalp and growth patterns. Here's how we approach them.

My hairline is receding or thinning

Temples pulling back or a sparse crown make long, heavy styles look thinner than they really are.

MY APPROACH

Shorter sides and a textured top that adds visual density, cut to work with your hairline, not against it.

Dandruff and a dry, itchy scalp

Flaking and itching are often made worse by harsh shampoos, hot water and Danish winters.

MY APPROACH

A scalp check during your consultation, plus honest advice on your washing routine and products that actually help.

A cowlick that won't lie flat

Crowns and fringes that stick up or split usually fight back because the cut ignores the growth direction.

MY APPROACH

I read the growth pattern and cut the weight so the hair falls naturally with the cowlick instead of against it.

Curly hair ends up frizzy

Curly and wavy hair puffs out or loses shape when it's cut like straight hair.

MY APPROACH

Curl-aware cutting and shaping, plus a simple at-home routine for defined, controlled texture.

Razor bumps after a fade

Red bumps and irritation on the neck and beard line are common after shaving or very close fades.

MY APPROACH

Correct blade direction, tools sanitised after every client, and a soothing finish for sensitive skin.

A patchy beard

Gaps on the cheeks or an uneven neckline can make a beard look untidy even when it's grown out.

MY APPROACH

Structured shaping and a clean line-up that disguise patches and balance your face shape.

Greasy and flat by midday

Oily roots weigh hair down, so it won't hold a style or any volume.

MY APPROACH

A cut that removes bulk at the root, plus matte products that hold without weighing your hair down.

My cut only lasts a week

If a cut isn't tailored to your head shape and growth, it loses its shape fast as it grows out.

MY APPROACH

A consultation-first cut, built around your face shape, so it keeps looking good between visits.
